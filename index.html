FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you take my site live on my domain straight away?
No. You get a Netlify preview first. Production publish and custom domain only after you (and the owner) approve and the balance is paid.
What’s included in the deposit?
50% of the package price to start. Balance before production go-live / domain attach. No payment plans or discounts without owner yes.
Are these prices final?
Not yet. Every figure on this site is PROPOSED until the owner types prices accepted. Quotes stay marked PROPOSED until then.
Will you invent reviews or client logos for me?
No. We do not invent clients, testimonials, or numbers. DEMO sites are clearly labelled fictional portfolio pieces.
What if I need more than one revision?
Spark includes 1 revision round after first preview. Launch and Engine include 2. Extra work is a change order — not silent overtime.
Do you write all the copy?
We tighten what you give us. Full copywriting beyond that is out of scope on Spark/Launch unless agreed in a change order.
Can you do medical, legal, crypto or adult sites?
No. Those are on the refuse list.
What about ongoing updates?
That’s Care — PROPOSED £149/month, up to 3 hours. Overage only after a change order, at £50/hr PROPOSED.
Who hosts it?
Netlify. Hosting fees billed by Netlify to you are outside our package price.
