FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a custom website?
No. It’s a template pack. You pick a skin; we fit your details. That’s how we keep the pack price and the timeline honest.
Can I see it before I pay the balance?
Yes. Everything starts on a Netlify preview. Production and custom domain only after you approve.
What if none of the skins fit?
We match the nearest trade skin and adjust colours/copy inside that system. Full bespoke layout is out of scope on Spark — that’s a change order, not the pack.
Are prices final?
Still PROPOSED until the owner types prices accepted.
Do you invent reviews for the DEMO?
DEMOs may use clearly labelled placeholder trust lines. We never invent real clients or testimonials for your live site.
Deposit?
50% to start. Balance before go-live.
What if I need more than one revision?
Spark includes 1 revision round after first preview. Launch and Engine include 2. Extra work is a change order — not silent overtime.
Can you do medical, legal, crypto or adult sites?
No. Those are on the refuse list.
What about ongoing updates?
That’s Care — PROPOSED £149/month, up to 3 hours. Overage only after a change order, at £50/hr PROPOSED.
Who hosts it?
Netlify. Hosting fees billed by Netlify to you are outside our pack price.
