FAQ

Straight answers.

Are these prices final?
No. Every price on this site is marked PROPOSED pending owner acceptance. Nothing is live until the owner confirms.
How do deposits work?
Build packages (Spark / Launch / Engine) need a 50% deposit to start. Balance is due before production go-live or domain attach. Care is billed monthly.
What counts as a revision?
A revision round is a bundled set of feedback after you see the Netlify preview — not unlimited back-and-forth. Spark includes 1; Launch and Engine include 2. Extra rounds are change-ordered.
Who owns the domain?
You do. We deploy to a Netlify preview first. Custom domains are only attached after owner approval — we do not configure domains from this enquiry form alone.
How long does a build take?
Timelines start after deposit + usable content (copy, photos, service list, areas covered). We do not promise calendar dates until those are in. Typical Spark budget is 6–8 hrs; Launch 14–20 hrs against the rate floor.
Do you write all the copy?
We do a tight rewrite of owner-supplied notes. Full copywriting from scratch, brand systems and photo shoots are out of scope unless quoted separately.
What is Care for?
Care is a retainer: up to 3 hours/month for updates, content tweaks and Netlify health checks on sites we built. Overage only after an approved change order at the £50/hr floor (PROPOSED).
Are the portfolio sites real clients?
No. Ashford Drain Co and Hedge & Order are labelled DEMO / fictional. They exist to show build quality — never as testimonials or case studies.
What niches do you refuse?
Medical/health claims, legal advice, crypto/gambling, adult, and anything needing regulated professional advice we cannot stand behind.
